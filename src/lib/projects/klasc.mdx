A workspace where chat, voice, and boards share one room. Channels stay readable when the day gets loud, a huddle hangs off the thread and leaves when it is done, and a decision in chat becomes a card without opening another tool. Each organization stays isolated: private channels, expiring invites, and files that are never public until someone shares them.
