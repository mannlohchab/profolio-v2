Research companion for a unified scoring-based heuristic for constraint-aware graph coloring, co-authored with Dr. R.P. Yadav at SRM University. Formulates coloring as score maximization with BFS separation, conflict penalties, and color regularization for wireless interference and multi-constraint networks.
